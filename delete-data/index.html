Delete your Highlights data

Highlights by Tamago Studio

You can delete your Highlights account and everything we store about you at any time, including if you signed in with Apple or Google.

In the app

  1. Open Highlights and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then tap the gear to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, type your username, then confirm. If you signed in with Apple, confirm with Apple once more.

This permanently deletes your account, profile, followers and following, and every Highlight and its photos from our servers, including Private ones and their backups. It also removes your account’s Highlights from that iPhone. If you signed in with Apple, you can also remove Highlights from your Apple ID in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple. It can’t be undone. A Highlights+ subscription isn’t cancelled by this; cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If you can’t use the app

Email support@tamago.studio from the email address on your account (or tell us which sign-in you used) and ask us to delete your data. We’ll confirm once it’s done, usually within a few days.
