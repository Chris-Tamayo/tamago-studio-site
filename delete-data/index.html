Delete your Highlights data

Highlights by Tamago Studio

You can delete your Highlights account and everything we store about you at any time, including if you signed in with Apple, Google or Facebook.

In the app

  1. Open Highlights and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then tap the gear to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, profile, followers and following, your shared Highlights and their photos from our servers, and removes your account’s Highlights from that iPhone. It can’t be undone.

If you can’t use the app

Email hello@tamago.studio from the email address on your account (or tell us which sign-in you used) and ask us to delete your data. We’ll confirm once it’s done, usually within a few days.

Signed in with Facebook?

You can also remove Highlights from your Facebook account under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites. That stops Highlights from using your Facebook sign-in; to delete your Highlights data itself, use the steps above.
