Highlights Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Highlights is an iPhone app by Tamago Studio for keeping your favorite photos as “Highlights” and, if you choose, sharing them with close friends and family. This policy explains what information Highlights uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

The short version

  • Highlights are Private by default: only you can see them. They’re backed up to your account so they come back on a new iPhone.
  • Highlights you set to Friends can also be seen by the friends you accepted.
  • There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share your information for advertising.
  • You can delete your account, and everything we store about you, at any time in Settings.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (stored only in scrambled, “hashed” form), or the account identifier from Sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only see the private relay address.
  • Profile: your name, username, an optional profile photo and an optional bio. Other signed-in people can see these so they can find you, except people you have blocked, who can’t see your profile photo. Signed-in people can also see how many followers you have and how many people you follow.
  • Highlights: for every Highlight, Private and Friends alike, we store a backup of the title, dates, the place name you typed, the texts you added, resized copies of the photos in it, the day each photo was taken, and how you arranged them. We don’t read or store your photos’ GPS location.
  • Friends: who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, and anyone you block.
  • Reports: if you report a person or Highlight, the reason, any details you write, and who sent it. The person you report isn’t told who reported them.
  • Highlights+: Apple handles payment. We never see your card or billing details, only whether your subscription is active.

Your photos

Highlights uses Apple’s photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you pick. It does not have access to the rest of your library. It keeps resized copies of picked photos inside the app and in your account’s backup. Deleting a Highlight never deletes anything from your Photos library.

Who can see what

  • Private: only you, in the app on any iPhone signed in to your account.
  • Friends: you, and people whose follow request you accepted.

Highlights has no public posts and no website for your Highlights, so nothing is visible on the web or to search engines.

These rules are enforced on our servers, not just in the app. Blocking someone hides all of your Highlights from them.

Backups are encrypted in storage and in transit, but not end to end. That means the people who run Highlights could technically access them. We don’t look at your Highlights, except to review something that was reported to us or when the law requires it.

How we use information

Only to run Highlights: to sign you in, back up your Highlights, show them to the people you chose, let friends find you by username, review reports, and send account emails (confirming your email address and resetting your password). We don’t send marketing emails.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts the account system, database and photo storage (servers in the United States).
  • Resend delivers account emails, so it receives your email address.
  • Apple, Google and Facebook, if you choose to sign in with them, confirm who you are and share your name and email address with us. We don’t post to them or read your contacts, friends lists or photos there.
  • Apple processes Highlights+ payments.

These services process information only to provide their service to us. Like most online services, they keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete a Highlight or your account, it’s removed from our servers right away, and from our hosting provider’s backups as they expire, within about 30 days. We keep reports as long as needed to review them and prevent repeat abuse.

Deleting your information

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account (details on our data deletion page). This permanently deletes your account, profile, follows, every Highlight and its photos from our servers, including Private ones, and removes the account’s Highlights from that iPhone. If you signed in with Apple, it also disconnects Highlights from your Apple ID. To remove a single Highlight, delete it. To stop friends seeing one, set it to Private; it disappears from their views the next time their app refreshes.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, and delete your account and everything in it at any time. To ask for a copy of your information or anything else about it, email us and we’ll answer within 30 days.

Children

Highlights is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above. For significant changes, we’ll let you know in the app.
