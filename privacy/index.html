Highlights Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Highlights is an iPhone app by Tamago Studio for keeping your favorite photos as “Highlights” and, if you choose, sharing them with close friends and family. This policy explains what information Highlights uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

The short version

  • Highlights are Private by default. Private Highlights stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Only Highlights you set to Friends or Public are uploaded, so the people you chose can see them.
  • There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete your account, and everything we store about you, at any time in Settings.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (stored only in scrambled, “hashed” form), or the account identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only see the private relay address.
  • Profile: your name, username, and an optional profile photo. Other signed-in people can see these so they can find you, except people you have blocked, who can’t see your profile photo.
  • Shared Highlights: for Highlights set to Friends or Public, we store the title, date, note, the place name you typed, and resized copies of the photos in it. We do not read your photos’ GPS location.
  • Friends: who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, and anyone you block.

Your photos

Highlights uses Apple’s photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you pick. It does not have access to the rest of your library. It keeps resized copies of picked photos inside the app. Deleting a Highlight never deletes anything from your Photos library.

Who can see what

  • Private: only you, on your iPhone.
  • Friends: you, and people whose follow request you accepted.
  • Public: anyone signed in to Highlights who visits your profile. Highlights has no website, so nothing is visible on the web or to search engines.

These rules are enforced on our servers, not just in the app. Blocking someone hides all of your Highlights from them.

How we use information

Only to run Highlights: to sign you in, show your Highlights to the people you chose, let friends find you by username, and send account emails (confirming your email address and resetting your password). We don’t send marketing emails.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts the account system, database and photo storage for shared Highlights.
  • Resend delivers account emails, so it receives your email address.
  • Apple and Google, if you choose to sign in with them, confirm who you are and share your name and email address with us.

These services process information only to provide their service to us. Like most online services, they keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Deleting your information

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, follows, shared Highlights and their photos from our servers, and removes the account’s Highlights from that iPhone. To remove a single shared Highlight, delete it or set it to Private. It disappears from other people’s views the next time their app refreshes.

Children

Highlights is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above. For significant changes, we’ll let you know in the app.
